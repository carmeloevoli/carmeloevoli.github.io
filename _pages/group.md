My research group at the Gran Sasso Science Institute works on the theory and phenomenology of
cosmic-ray transport, from Galactic propagation and non-linear feedback to the origin of
ultra-high-energy cosmic rays. This page lists current and former members, regular collaborators,
and the experimental collaborations I belong to.

## Journal club ##

The group runs [HAPPJ](/happj/), the High-energy AstroParticle Physics Journal Club, which meets
weekly at GSSI to discuss work in progress and recent literature. Students, postdocs and faculty
from GSSI take part, together with visitors from other institutes. Attendance is open; details and
the mailing list are on the [HAPPJ page](/happj/).

## Experimental collaborations ##

+ **[Pierre Auger Observatory](https://www.auger.org)** (2022–present) — member of the L'Aquila
group, working on mass composition and the energy spectrum at the highest energies.

+ **[Cherenkov Telescope Array](https://www.ctao.org)** (2015–2022) — member of the L'Aquila group.

Publications signed by these collaborations carry several hundred authors and are listed separately
from my own work on the [publications page](/publications/).
